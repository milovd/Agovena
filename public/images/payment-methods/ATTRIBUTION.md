# Payment method logo assets

These local SVG assets are used only as payment-method brand identifiers in Agovena Admin and checkout.

- The Stripe-compatible payment method SVG assets in this directory are from `@exisia/medusa-payment-icons` version `0.0.4`, published with an MIT license. Source repository: https://gitlab.com/exisia/medusajs/packages/medusa-payment-icons. No package dependency is installed at runtime.
- `creditcard.svg`, `card.svg`, `acss_debit.svg`, `multibanco.svg`, `pay_by_bank.svg` and `sofort.svg` are adapted from the MIT-licensed Siteation payment icon collection: https://github.com/Siteation/magento2-icons-payment.
- `giropay.svg` is the Giropay logo from Wikimedia Commons, sourced from `giropay.de` and attributed to paydirekt GmbH.
- `link.svg` is the Link logo published on https://link.com/ and remains a provider trademark.
- `twint.svg`, `crypto.svg` and `swish.svg` are sourced from the DataTrans payment logos collection, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0: https://github.com/datatrans/payment-logos.
- `zip.svg` is the Zip Co logo from Wikimedia Commons, sourced from Zip Co, and licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Zip_Logo.svg.

The assets are not used as payment authorization or provider API data.
